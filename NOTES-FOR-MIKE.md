## 4. Setup

### The estimate form

The form sends to **sudsawayprowash@yahoo.com** through FormSubmit. If it has never been activated,
**the first submission is used up by activation** and doesn't arrive as an enquiry:

1. Send one test from the contact page.
2. Look for an email from FormSubmit (**check spam**) and click its confirm link.
3. Send a second test. That one should arrive within a minute or two.

A working enquiry arrives with a subject like
`[SudsAway Web] House Washing + Roof Cleaning · Zionsville — Dana Whitfield`, a table of the
details, and the page they asked from. Hitting Reply goes straight to the customer.

**Make sure none get lost:** in Yahoo Mail, add a filter for subjects containing `[SudsAway Web]`
that moves them to a folder you check, so they never land in spam.

The email address shown on the site can be changed in the admin, but the form always delivers to
the Yahoo address, because FormSubmit activation is per address. If you switch email addresses,
let us know so the form can be moved and re-activated.
